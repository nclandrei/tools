Train your ear with recordings from Xeno-Canto's library of ~1M wildlife sounds. Pick a region, listen, guess the species.

One-time setup

This tool streams recordings from Xeno-Canto, the world's largest open library of wildlife sounds. Their API v3 needs a personal key — free, takes about a minute.

  1. Create a free account at xeno-canto.org/account/register
  2. Verify your email, then open your account page
  3. Copy your API key and paste it below

Your key is stored only in this browser's localStorage. It never leaves your device except to call Xeno-Canto directly.
